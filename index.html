Vantage

Scrabble on an impossible monument

  1. Earn letters in curved space. In the Hyperbolic Chamber, space stretches towards the rim. Pick the crystal that is truly closest.
  2. Find the vantage point. Strips of tiles blocks apart in 3D line up on screen from just the right angle, like a Monument Valley level.
  3. Spell across the illusion. Type LOVE on the plaza and, from the right corner, it runs on into ABLE on a floating arm: LOVEABLE. Joined words score once per surface.

Best with sound · About 10 minutes · Words checked with Merriam-Webster's Scrabble dictionary

Hyperbolic Chamber

Hyperbolic Lab

You are the white dot, always at the centre: the plane slides past you. Every floor tile is the same size; the plane only looks squeezed near the rim. The star is home, and a red arrow is painted there pointing north.

W A S D or arrow keys to walk · Hold the floor to walk towards it · Click to drop a triangle corner

Try this

  1. Walk a square. Do you get home?
  2. Drop three corners. Can the angles reach 180°?
  3. Walk out towards the rim. Does it ever get closer?
  4. Walk a big loop back to the star. Which way does the arrow point now?
