      Vantage

      Scrabble on an impossible monument

      1. Earn letters in curved space. Play a quick game on a floor that looks squished near the rim: grab crystals, putt along curved lines, dodge shadows, then solve one puzzle. More stars, better letters.
      2. Press Isometric view. Some rows of tiles are far apart in 3D, but from one special angle they look like a single line, like a Monument Valley level.
      3. Click a glowing tile and type a word. Start on the plaza and let the word run across the gap into the letters on a floating arm. Words that cross the gap score double, and the traveller walks them.

            Hyperbolic Chamber

            Hyperbolic Lab

            You are the white dot, always at the centre: the world slides past you and you never turn. The star is home, with a red arrow painted there pointing north.

            W A S D or arrow keys to walk · Hold the floor to walk towards it · Click to drop a triangle corner

            Try this

            1. Walk a square. Do you get home? Now switch geometry and try again.
            2. Drop three corners. Can the angles reach 180°?
            3. Walk out towards the rim. Does it ever get closer?
            4. Walk a big loop back to the star. Which way does the arrow point now?
